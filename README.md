## About YourNeed

YourNeed is a smart home-service connection platform designed to connect people who need home repair services with skilled service providers who are looking for work.

The platform helps customers quickly find service professionals for:

- Electrical repairs
- Fan and AC repairs
- Refrigerator repairs
- Plumbing services
- Washing machine repairs
- Home appliance maintenance
- Other household repair services

## Main Objective

Solve two major problems:

1. Customers struggle to find reliable service providers quickly.
2. Skilled technicians struggle to find customers and regular work opportunities.

YourNeed brings both sides together through one convenient digital platform.
